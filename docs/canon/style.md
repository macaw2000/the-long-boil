# House style

Source: `docs/gdd.md`, section 2, "Tone and voice". These rules are enforced by `scripts/validate/` where they can be, and by the inspector where they cannot.

## Voice

Kettleport is grim and funny in the same breath.

- Second person, present tense. "You", never "the player".
- Short and specific. A No. 4 spanner beats an old tool.
- Dry wit over gore. Terrible things happen offstage and are described by their aftermath.
- Victorian register without pastiche: no "forsooth", no modern slang, no real-world politics.
- Nobody explains the mystery. Truth arrives in fragments: a ledger page, a whistle note, two gauges that disagree.

## Limits

| Field | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Storylet description | 120 words |
| Each option label | 25 words |
| Each outcome | 150 words |
| Item flavour text | 1 sentence (longer belongs to the scrivener, not the draftsman) |

## Banned

"tapestry", "testament to", "delve", "a sense of", "little did you know", and any sentence that explains its own joke.

## Options

Every storylet gives a real choice. If one option is obviously correct, it is not a choice and the scene needs rewriting. Challenge options show their odds before the player commits; there are no hidden rolls.
