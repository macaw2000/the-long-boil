# The reveal schedule, Layers 1 to 3

Source: `docs/gdd.md`, section 4.

Players learn the truth in layers. Each layer belongs to an act, and a storylet tagged with a layer cannot sit in an earlier act. The `reveal_guard` validator enforces the act; the inspector catches the hinting that code cannot see.

This file stops at Layer 3. Layers 4 and 5 are in `locked.md` and are not available to any agent.

| Layer | The truth | Act | Delivered through |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | The official story. The Seven Gauges lifted the town off a boiling sea and the Board keeps it rising. | All | `world.md` |
| 1 | The Board posts false Gauge Readings. The Scald has risen 70 fathoms in 11 years, not the 6 the Gazette admits. | I | Ledger Page 1, from Scrapjaw's chest |
| 2 | The Great Kettle draws heat up the Downpipe from an engine on the seabed, the Hearth. Drawing on it is what boils the sea. The city is the Boil. | II | Old Bellows' last breath, and Ledger Page 9 |
| 3 | People live down in the Scald: the Steeped, descendants of those left behind at the Lift. | III | Brass capsules knocking up the Downpipe |

## Writing against a layer

**Cleared for Layer 1**: you may write suspicion of the Board's numbers, a gauge that disagrees, a district that knows it is being lied to. You may not write heat coming up the pipe, anything burning on the seabed, or any suggestion that the city causes the Scald.

**Cleared for Layer 2**: all of the above, plus the Hearth and the direction the heat travels. You may not write anyone living down there, or anything knocking.

**Cleared for Layer 3**: all of the above, plus the Steeped as a fact. You may not write where the Hearth came from, who built it, or why. That is Layer 4 and it is locked.

## Hinting counts

A scene that avoids stating an uncleared fact but is clearly built around it is a leak. A character who is suspiciously careful not to answer a question is a leak. If the scene cannot work without the fact, do not write the scene: raise it.
