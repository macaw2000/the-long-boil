# Slice: Act II, What the Furnace Eats

Attach this file, plus `world.md`, `characters.md`, `style.md`, `qualities.json`, and `act1.md` for continuity. Attach nothing above it.

**Clearance: Layer 2.** See `reveals.md`, truncated at Layer 2.

## Where it happens

Cinderworks Foundry (levels 20 to 45), the Lowvents, the Chapel of the Steady Flame, Gaugehill.

## The act in one line

The Kettle does not ride the steam. It pulls it up from somewhere, and the furnaces want more than coal.

## Beats, in order

1. **Shift work.** A Stokers' shift in the Cinderworks. Soot Wraiths are shapes in the smoke, mostly harmless until they are not. The Chapel calls them the faithful dead rising; the Stokers call them the furnace's breath.
2. **The wrong note.** The Great Whistle sounds off the hour. Haze enters the game here: every storylet that listens to the Whistle raises it.
3. **The hymnal.** Hale opens the Chapel archive to Tinkers with enough Favour or a fine enough tea. The oldest hymn's notation matches the off-hour note exactly.
4. **What the Stokers know.** Mother Ash admits the Stokers have always heard words in the Whistle. The commonest one is "enough".
5. **Below the Kettle-heart.** A maintenance hatch leads to the top of the Downpipe. The heat is coming up, not going down. Something is burning on the seabed.
6. **Old Bellows.** The Foundry's first bellows-engine. Its last breath runs through every pipe; players holding 3 or more Whistle notations can translate it: "The fire was lent. The loan is due."
7. **The page.** Ledger Page 9, one line in Hester's hand: "The Kettle isn't lifting us off a boiling sea. The Kettle is boiling the sea."
8. **Ballast Day is called.** The Board names the Lowvents. Guilds begin choosing sides. Hester's last page in the act says she went down the Downpipe in a borrowed storm-proof gondola.

## New in this act

Haze, the Chapel, Whistle notations, the Downpipe's upper hatch, the Reckoning opening.

## Hard limits for this slice

- The translated line "The fire was lent. The loan is due." may be **quoted as heard and left unexplained**. Who lent it, what the Hearth is for, and what the loan means are Layer 4 and locked. No character speculates toward an answer.
- No Steeped, nothing living below, nothing knocking, no capsules. That is Act III.
- The Baron and the Thunderhead may be named as a place and a rumour, not met.
