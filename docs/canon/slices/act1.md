# Slice: Act I, The Gauge Lies

Attach this file, plus `world.md`, `characters.md`, `style.md` and `qualities.json`, to any Act I work order. Attach nothing else.

**Clearance: Layer 1.** See `reveals.md`, which the agent receives truncated at Layer 1.

## Where it happens

Rustmoor Scrapyards (levels 1 to 20), Sprocket Row, the Polytechnic, the Lowvents, the Brass Exchange.

## The act in one line

Someone is lying about how high the city flies, and Hester Vane found out.

## Beats, in order

1. **The Altitude Review.** The Gazette announces a routine review of the city's altitude. In the Lowvents, "review" means Ballast. Pip's family is on a list that does not officially exist.
2. **The sealed laboratory.** Hester's lab is locked and Brassbuckle will not discuss it. A Tinkering check opens a drawer: an index card reading "R.S. The Rats have it."
3. **Rubbish with signatures.** Everything thrown off Gaugehill lands in Rustmoor, Board paperwork included. Scavenging turns up torn gauge charts in the Rust Rats' nests.
4. **A vent in the Lowvents.** Mother Ash wants a broken vent fixed. From the bottom rim you take your own reading of the Scald, and it disagrees with the Gazette by 40 fathoms.
5. **Notice.** Inspector Rook starts appearing wherever you have just been. Cooperating makes her an ally with questions; evading raises Notice.
6. **Scrapjaw.** The Rust Rats' king, built from three centuries of rubbish. Drops Scrapjaw's Cleaver and Ledger Page 1.
7. **The page.** Hester kept two columns: the Board's readings and her own. 70 fathoms in 11 years against the Gazette's 6.
8. **What you do with it.** Give it to Inkwell, Rook or Gauntlett, or keep it. Each raises one faction's Favour and colours later scenes. None locks content.

The act ends with the player choosing an Ambition, and holding a Foundry Pass by one of three roads: a Stokers' token, a Board pass, or a pass bought from Tallow.

## Cast available

Brassbuckle, Pip Tumbler, Mother Ash, Inspector Rook, Silas Tallow, Mrs. Inkwell, Lord Gauntlett. Hester only through Ledger Pages.

## Needles in play

Scald and Arrears from the Prologue. Notice from beat 5. **Haze does not exist yet**: it enters in Act II with the Whistle. Do not reference it.
