# Slice: Act III, Lightning Down

Attach this file, plus `world.md`, `characters.md`, `style.md`, `qualities.json`, and `act1.md` and `act2.md` for continuity.

**Clearance: Layer 3.** See `reveals.md`, truncated at Layer 3.

## Where it happens

Thunderhead Reach (levels 45 to 70), the Baron's aerie, Gaugehill, the Downpipe's upper hatch.

## The act in one line

The Lightning Baron offers the city a new fire, and you need his gondola to follow Hester down.

## Beats, in order

1. **The kite-lift.** Thunderhead Reach is reached by kite. The Baron's invitation arrives before you do.
2. **The pitch.** Strake taps storms and wants to run Kettleport on lightning, with himself in the Board's chairs. Charming, half right, offers a partnership.
3. **Storm Harpies.** The taps breed them. Every tap you help build makes the zone more crowded.
4. **Knocking.** Lightning drawn down through the Scald sets something knocking in the Downpipe. Brass capsules come up carrying letters in an old dialect. The Steeped exist, and the storms are falling on them.
5. **The Baron's answer.** Told about the Steeped, he reacts to your earlier choices: guilt if you partnered with him, contempt if you did not.
6. **The Lightning Baron.** The semester's final boss. Drops the Coil Staff and a bound Gondola Schematic.
7. **The last capsule.** In Hester's handwriting: "They're alive down here. Don't let the Board finish the Dreadnought." The same day, the Gazette's front page shows the Iron Dreadnought, built to go down the Downpipe and seal it from below. The capstone ends here.

## New in this act

The Steeped as a fact, the capsules and their dialect, the Gondola Schematic, the Dreadnought as a headline.

## Hard limits for this slice

- The Steeped may be shown as people who are alive, who write, and who are being rained on. Where they came from beyond "left behind at the Lift", what the Hearth is, who built it and why are Layer 4 and locked.
- Hester is a voice on paper only. She is not met, not seen, not confirmed alive in person.
- The Dreadnought is a newspaper picture and a threat. Its interior, crew and launch are Act IV.
- **The Steeped's dialect is not written out.** The GDD says the capsules carry letters in an old dialect and does not supply it. Inventing one is a canon decision for Jeremy. Open question.
