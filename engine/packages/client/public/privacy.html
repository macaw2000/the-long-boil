Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

The Long Boil is an online game at thelongboil.com, made and run by Jeremy Rule ("we", "us"). This policy says what we collect when you play, why, who else handles it, and what you can ask us to do with it. The game is in development, and we will update this page when what we collect changes.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in. You sign in with Google or Discord. Our sign-in service (Amazon Cognito) receives and keeps your email address, whether it is verified, and your first name from Google or your username from Discord. The game itself stores only an account identifier derived from your sign-in, not your email or name.
  • Your game. Your Tinkers' names, skills, items, Crowns, queued work, story choices, trades on the in-game Exchange, guild membership, and a log of the game events that produced them.
  • Chat. Messages you send in the game's chat channels and whispers, with the time and the Tinker who sent them, and the players you have muted.
  • Purchases. If you buy Cog Crates, Stripe processes the payment. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us. We keep the Stripe customer identifier for your account and a record of which crates each payment delivered, so a payment is honoured exactly once and a refund or dispute can be matched to it.
  • Technical logs. Our servers write logs of requests and errors, which can include your IP address and browser details. They are kept for 14 days.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking services, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

On your device

The game keeps your sign-in tokens in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in, and holds a short-lived value in session storage while a sign-in is in progress. It may also remember small preferences, such as which tab you had open. The sign-in pages set their own cookies to complete a sign-in. Signing out removes the game's tokens.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account and your Tinkers yours.
  • To run the game: progress while you are away, the shared Exchange, guilds and chat.
  • To deliver what you buy, and to handle refunds and disputes.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: enforcing the Terms of Service, acting on reports, and fixing faults.

Who else handles it

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the game, its database and its sign-in service, in the United States (Oregon).
  • Google and Discord, when you choose to sign in with them, under their own privacy policies.
  • Stripe processes payments, under its own privacy policy.

Other players see your Tinkers' names, your chat messages, your guild, and what you trade on the Exchange. They never see your email address.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect players or the game from fraud or abuse.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data while your account exists. Technical logs are deleted after 14 days. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require, even after an account is deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and its Tinkers. Write to macaw2000@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, and we will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, such as the GDPR or the CCPA; write to us to use them.

Children

The Long Boil is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will also say so in the game.

Contact

Questions about this policy: macaw2000@gmail.com.
